Terms of use

Last updated 19 November 2026

These terms cover VocaLink: the website at this address, which translates speech in your browser, and the VocaLink bot for Discord. By using either, you agree to them.

What VocaLink does

VocaLink turns speech into text and translates it as people talk. In the browser it uses your own Deepgram key. In Discord, the bot listens in the voice channel it is started in, and posts captions and personal translations for the people in that call.

Using it fairly

  • Tell the people in a call that it is being translated. The bot posts a notice when it starts, and anyone can stop their own voice being processed with /optout.
  • Do not use VocaLink to record, monitor or impersonate anyone, or for anything illegal or against Discord's own terms.
  • Only clone your own voice. The bot only does this after you choose it yourself, and you can delete it at any time.

Plans and payment

The Discord bot has a free plan and paid plans per server. Paid plans are sold and billed by Discord through its app shop, and Discord's own payment and refund rules apply. You can cancel at any time in Discord's settings; the plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Monthly hours reset at the start of each calendar month (UTC) and do not roll over.

Accuracy

Machine transcription and translation make mistakes, especially with names, slang and noisy audio. Do not rely on VocaLink where a mistake could cause harm, such as medical, legal or safety conversations.

Availability

VocaLink depends on outside services (Discord, Deepgram, Soniox, ElevenLabs and Google Translate). We try to keep it running, but it is provided as it is, without a guarantee that it will always be available or error free. We may change or stop features, and will say so on this site.
