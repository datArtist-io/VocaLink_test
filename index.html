    VocaLink for Discord

    Add the bot to your server and everyone in the voice channel hears the others in their own language, about a second behind. Captions go to a thread, and each person gets a private translator that never plays their own voice back.

    1. Add VocaLink to your server with the button below.
    2. Pick your languages. An admin runs /setup and chooses up to three.
    3. Start talking. Join a voice channel and run /translate start. Everyone taps Open my translator and hears the others in their own language.
