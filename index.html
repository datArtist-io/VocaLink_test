How it works

Four steps, and none of them are yours to manage.

Once VocaLink is in the channel, the whole loop runs on its own. Nobody sets a language and nobody presses translate.

01

Someone starts talking

In whatever language they actually speak. There's no picker and nothing to configure first.

02

VocaLink identifies it

The language is detected from the speech itself, sentence by sentence.

03

It's translated

Into the server's target language: English, Spanish, French and seven more, switching in real time.

04

You see or hear it

Captions post right away. Spoken dubbing is optional and only uses a voice its owner enrolled.

What it does

Built around how a live conversation actually moves.

10 languages, automatically

Nobody has to tell VocaLink what's being spoken. It listens and works it out sentence by sentence, switching between English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian and Dutch, even mid-conversation.

Captions that keep up

Translated text lands in the channel within a couple of seconds of someone pausing. That's fast enough to follow a live conversation instead of reading it back later.

Voices, only by consent

Anyone can enroll their own voice so their translated speech is spoken back in it. Only they can do that for themselves, and nobody else's voice is used without them opting in.

Already where you're talking

No separate app and nothing for anyone to download. VocaLink joins the voice channel you're already in.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does VocaLink store what people say?

Audio is processed into a transcript and translation in the moment and isn't kept afterward. Voice samples for cloned dubbing are stored only for the person who enrolled them, and they can delete them anytime.

How many languages does it support?

VocaLink currently auto-detects and switches between 10 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian and Dutch. Expanding toward 100+ is on the roadmap, and this page will change as languages go live.

Do I need to be a server admin to add it?

Yes. Adding any bot to a Discord server requires the Manage Server permission.

Can I hear translations in my own voice?

Only if you enroll your voice yourself with explicit consent. Nobody can enroll your voice on your behalf.

What happens if the subscription ends?

The server drops back to the Free tier's limits. Nothing is deleted; translation returns to the free monthly cap.

What permissions does VocaLink need?

Connect and Speak in voice channels, and Send Messages in whichever text channel you choose for captions.
