Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

VocaLink is built so that what people say is used once, to translate it, and then let go.

In your browser (this website)

  • Audio goes straight from your browser to Deepgram, using the key you paste. It does not pass through a VocaLink server.
  • Each finished sentence is sent to Google Translate, or to Anthropic's Claude if you add your own key, to be translated.
  • Your keys, settings and speaker names are stored only in your browser. The transcript stays on the page until you clear it or close the tab, unless you save it yourself.

In Discord (the bot)

  • While translation is on in a voice channel, the bot receives the voices in that channel and sends them to Soniox to be transcribed and translated, and the translations to ElevenLabs when the server's plan includes AI voices.
  • Audio is never recorded or written to disk. Captions are posted in a Discord thread, where they stay like any other message.
  • The bot stores each server's settings, each person's language and voice choices, and how many minutes the server has used this month. It keeps usage counts without any of the words.
  • Anyone can run /optout. Their voice is then ignored before it is processed, in every server.

Your own voice

If you choose "My own voice", the bot keeps about 45 seconds of your clear speech in memory, sends it once to ElevenLabs to create the voice, and discards it. "Delete my voice" or /optout deletes the voice at ElevenLabs straight away.

Payments

Paid plans are bought through Discord's app shop. Discord handles the payment; VocaLink only learns which servers have which plan, never card details.

Services we rely on

Deepgram, Soniox, ElevenLabs, Google Translate and Anthropic each process the text or audio sent to them under their own privacy terms. Discord's privacy policy covers your use of Discord.

Contact

To ask about your data or have it removed, open an issue on VocaLink's GitHub page.
